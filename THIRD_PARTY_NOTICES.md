# Third-party notices

The Blender add-on and reconstruction bridge are GPL-3.0-or-later.
The separately installed runtime uses these upstream projects; their licenses
remain in their packages in the runtime's node_modules directory:

- [convert-pax](https://github.com/AndreBaltazar8/convert-pax): MIT.
- [spec-pax](https://github.com/AndreBaltazar8/spec-pax): MIT.
- [Sharp](https://github.com/lovell/sharp): Apache-2.0; bundled libvips and its
  dependencies have their own notices distributed by Sharp.
- Other transitive dependencies, including meshoptimizer and Basis Universal,
  retain the licenses supplied in their respective distributions.

The installable ZIP includes a pinned dependency lockfile, not native codec
binaries. Runtime installation retrieves the dependency packages and notices.

## Test fixtures

`tests/fixtures/compatibility.glb` is the generated MIT test fixture from
[convert-pax at 14f172a](https://github.com/AndreBaltazar8/convert-pax/tree/14f172a158c19933fa74119602641b272aee9673).
`tests/fixtures/basis.glb` uses generated geometry from that project and a texture
from Khronos's [FlightHelmet sample](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/FlightHelmet),
released under CC0 (public-domain source; conversion by Gary Hsu). These fixtures are only in the source repository,
not the add-on ZIP. The procedural Blender test scene is generated by this project.
